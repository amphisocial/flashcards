Pulley & Mechanical-Advantage Simulation

Add a pulley, feel the force needed drop — the trade between force and distance made visible.

See the teaching outline

Opens a live board in your browser. No install, no hardware.

A block on a ramp: change the angle, friction and mass and watch what actually moves.

Mechanical advantage is one of those ideas that lands the moment students can pull the rope themselves. AthenaBoard turns your sketch of a pulley system into an interactive model where the force-vs-distance trade is something the class changes and measures, not something they copy off a slide.

45-minute outline

How to teach it

  1. Hook (5 min): ask why a single fixed pulley makes lifting feel easier — collect guesses.
  2. Model (10 min): sketch the system on the board; AthenaBoard renders the forces.
  3. Explore (15 min): add pulleys and watch required force halve while the rope you pull doubles.
  4. Formalize (10 min): Analyze writes out mechanical advantage; push a quick check to students.
