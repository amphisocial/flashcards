Flat, concave and convex mirrors. A curvature slider moves the focus.

Reflection is easy to state and hard to picture. AthenaBoard draws the rays for you: flat mirrors keep them parallel, concave mirrors pull them to a real focus at f = R/2, convex mirrors spread them from a virtual one. Change the curvature and the focus slides with it.

45-minute outline

How to teach it

  1. Recall (5 min): angle of incidence = angle of reflection — sketch it.
  2. Show (10 min): send parallel rays at a flat mirror, then a concave one.
  3. Explore (15 min): students move the curvature slider and locate the focus.
  4. Apply (10 min): where does the focus go on a convex mirror? Analyze and check.
