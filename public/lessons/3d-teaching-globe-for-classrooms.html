A flat map distorts everything; a globe fixes it, but you can only own one and only one student can spin it. AthenaBoard puts a real teaching globe on every screen — satellite or political, with country borders, capitals, rivers, the equator and tropics, and labels that reveal themselves as students zoom in.

45-minute outline

How to teach it

  1. Orient (5 min): find the equator and the tropics; why are they there?
  2. Explore (15 min): students zoom into a continent and read the capitals that appear.
  3. Compare (10 min): switch between satellite and political views of the same region.
  4. Check (10 min): a quick map-reading check pushed to every device.
