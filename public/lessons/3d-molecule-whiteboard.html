3D Molecule Whiteboard

Name or draw a compound; rotate the ball-and-stick model and zoom into an atom's electron shells.

See the teaching outline

Opens a live board in your browser. No install, no hardware.

Acetic acid, CH₃COOH. Drag to rotate; click an atom for its electron shells.

A molecule drawn flat on a board hides the thing that matters: its shape. AthenaBoard turns a name or a sketch into a rotatable 3D model with correct bonds, then lets a student click any atom to fly into its electron shells — protons, shells, and valence electrons, all labeled.

45-minute outline

How to teach it

  1. Recall (5 min): what does a structural formula not tell us? (Shape.)
  2. Build (10 min): name a compound; rotate the model as a class.
  3. Zoom in (15 min): click an atom, count the valence electrons, connect to bonding.
  4. Check (10 min): Analyze the structure and push a short check.
