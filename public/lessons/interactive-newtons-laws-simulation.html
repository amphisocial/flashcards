Interactive Newton's Laws Simulation

Change the force and the mass — watch acceleration respond, live, in front of the class.

See the teaching outline

Opens a live board in your browser. No install, no hardware.

Drop a feather and a stone in real gravity. Toggle air resistance; switch to Moon gravity.

Students memorize F = ma without ever feeling it. On AthenaBoard you write the equation by hand, hit Analyze, and it becomes a running simulation the whole class can push on. Turn air resistance off and the feather and the hammer land together — the Apollo-15 moment, on a Chromebook.

45-minute outline

How to teach it

  1. Warm-up (5 min): ask the class to predict which lands first — a feather or a stone. Take a vote.
  2. Reveal (10 min): run the drop with air resistance ON, then OFF. Let a student change gravity to the Moon.
  3. Explore (15 min): students on their own screens change mass and force and describe what happens to acceleration.
  4. Check (10 min): tap Analyze for the worked F = ma steps, then push a 3-question check to every device.
